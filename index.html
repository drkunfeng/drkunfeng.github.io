About Me

I am a Lecturer in Civil Engineering at Anglia Ruskin University (ARU), based at ARU Peterborough. I received my PhD in Civil Engineering from University College Dublin in 2022 and previously worked as a Research Fellow at Queen's University Belfast.

My research focuses on structural health monitoring (SHM) of bridges and transport infrastructure, with particular interests in indirect or drive-by sensing, vehicle–bridge interaction, bridge scour, and data-driven infrastructure assessment.

Research Interests

Structural Health Monitoring

Monitoring and assessment of civil infrastructure using vibration, sensing and signal processing.

Drive-by Bridge Monitoring

Vehicle-based sensing and vehicle–bridge interaction for scalable infrastructure assessment.

Bridge Scour & Resilience

Monitoring strategies for bridges exposed to environmental and structural hazards.

AI & Digital Infrastructure

Machine learning, digital twins and data-driven decision support for infrastructure systems.
