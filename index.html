About

I am a Lecturer in Civil Engineering at Anglia Ruskin University and a member of its Centre for Engineering and Architectural Sustainability (CEAS).

I completed my PhD in structural health monitoring at University College Dublin (2018–2022), then worked as a Research Fellow at Queen’s University Belfast (2022–2024) on drive-by bridge monitoring within the EPSRC StreetZero project. I joined Anglia Ruskin in 2024.

Research

Drive-by structural health monitoring

Identifying bridge frequencies, damage and support conditions from sensors on passing vehicles instead of on the bridge.

Vehicle–bridge interaction

Modelling and inverse methods that separate vehicle, road profile and bridge contributions in measured responses.

Bridge weigh-in-motion

Estimating vehicle weights from bridge responses, and quantifying how uncertain those estimates are.

Recent work

My recent papers look at drive-by monitoring of bridge support stiffness, fleet monitoring with operational deflection shapes, and whether bridge weigh-in-motion weights are needed for damage detection.
